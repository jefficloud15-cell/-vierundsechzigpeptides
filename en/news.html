Clinical trialsNov 16, 2026

Semaglutide in children aged 6 to 11: BMI falls 13.5% in the STEP Young trial

Published in NEJM and presented at ObesityWeek 2026. Across 167 participants, BMI fell 13.5% on semaglutide and rose 2.7% on placebo at 68 weeks. Gastrointestinal effects were the most common.

RegulationBefore Feb 2027 Upcoming

GHK-Cu, LL-37 and three other peptides go before an FDA committee by February

The Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) has not set a date. On the list: GHK-Cu, LL-37, dihexa, melanotan II and PEG-MGF. FDA moved GHK-Cu to its Category 1 in May 2026.

Source: Latham & WatkinsRelated: What are research peptides?
RegulationOct 29, 2026

The US opens an interim path for compounding with six peptides

HHS will not act against licensed pharmacies that compound BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, MOTS-c, epitalon and Semax under individual prescriptions while FDA settles its 503A list. It is not an approval: the policy is interim and can be revoked.

Context: It applies to US pharmacies filling prescriptions. It does not change the status of materials sold for research (RUO).

Source: AJMC · RAPSRelated: What are research peptides?
IndustryAug 12, 2026

Lilly sues six companies for selling retatrutide labelled 'research use only' for human use

The drugmaker alleges the products were labelled RUO but promoted for people. It says it has reported more than 200 entities and over 14,000 listings.

Context: An RUO label only means something if the product is genuinely sold and promoted for the lab.

Source: HealioRelated: What are research peptides?
RegulationJul 23–24, 2026

FDA committee recommends adding six peptides to the 503A list

PCAC voted for BPC-157, KPV, TB-500, MOTS-c, epitalon and Semax, and against emideltide. The vote is advisory: the final decision goes through HHS and rulemaking.

Source: NCPARelated: What are research peptides?
Clinical trialsJul 23, 2026

Retatrutide also meets its goals in TRIUMPH-2 and TRIUMPH-3

At 80 weeks: up to −20.8% body weight in adults with type 2 diabetes, with A1C down by up to 1.6 points, and up to −22.6% in severe obesity with cardiovascular disease.

Source: Eli LillyRelated: Retatrutide vs. tirzepatide
Clinical trialsMay 21, 2026

TRIUMPH-1: retatrutide reaches −28.3% body weight at 80 weeks

First phase 3 trial of the triple agonist (GIP, GLP-1 and glucagon), with 2,339 participants. The placebo group lost 2.2%.

Source: Eli LillyRelated: Retatrutide vs. tirzepatide
RegulationApr 1, 2026

FDA approves Foundayo (orforglipron), a GLP-1 agonist pill that is not a peptide

It is a once-daily small molecule with no food or water restrictions. In the ATTAIN-1 trial, the highest dose reached −12.4% body weight versus −0.9% on placebo.
