Analytical science

Accuracy

Exactitud

Agreement of a result with an accepted reference value, distinct from repeatability between measurements.

Source: ICH Q2(R2): Validation of Analytical Procedures (EMA)

Pharmacology

Affinity

Afinidad

Tendency of a ligand to associate with its target. It does not by itself measure the magnitude of the response.

Source: Neubig et al. (2003), IUPHAR terminology, PMID 14657418

Pharmacology

Agonist

Agonista

Ligand that produces a receptor-mediated response in a specified system.

Source: Neubig et al. (2003), IUPHAR terminology, PMID 14657418

Amino acid

Aminoácido

Molecule containing amino and carboxyl functions; alpha-amino acids form the residues of protein chains.

Analytical specificity

Especificidad analítica

Ability of a procedure to measure or identify the analyte in the presence of other potentially interfering substances.

Antagonist

Antagonista

Ligand that limits an agonist response; a neutral antagonist does not itself change the measured basal activity.

Prospective study in people evaluating intervention effects on defined outcomes. Its existence does not imply regulatory authorization.

Nonprotein organic component participating in enzyme-catalysed reactions; NAD+ is an example.

EC50

Concentration producing half the defined maximum response in an assay; it does not establish a safe exposure.

GPCR

Half-life

Vida media

Time for a quantity or concentration to fall by half under defined conditions. Not necessarily the duration of an effect.

HPLC

HPLC

Ligando

Molecule binding to a molecular target; that interaction does not necessarily activate it.

Biochemistry

NAD+

NAD+

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide, a coenzyme in redox reactions and substrate of other enzymes. It is not a peptide.

NADH
