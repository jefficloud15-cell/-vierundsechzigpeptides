Beach heat: store it right away

Coastal temperatures regularly reach 30 °C or more. Put vials in the fridge as soon as they arrive — or the freezer for long-term storage — and never leave a reconstituted vial in a car. Need the math for preparation? Use the reconstitution calculator.
